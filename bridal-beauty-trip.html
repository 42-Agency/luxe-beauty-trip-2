Treatments for Your Wedding Skin

The most important thing about wedding beauty prep is timing. Seoul gives you access to treatments that produce real, lasting results. The key is booking your trip the right number of months before your wedding so that the results have fully settled by the time you walk down the aisle.

When to Come

6+ months before
Best for lifting
Ultrasound lifting treatments (HIFU and Ultherapy) take 3 to 6 months to show their full effect. Book this far out for the most dramatic difference on your wedding day.
3 to 6 months
Ideal window
The sweet spot for most brides. Enough time for lifting results to peak, skin quality to improve, and any minor sensitivity to fully resolve before the big day.
6 to 8 weeks
Skin quality focus
Skin boosters and brightening treatments work well closer to the wedding. Great for a glow reset if the timing does not allow for a longer lead time.

What Korean Clinics Offer That Yours Do Not

✦

Rejuran (PDRN Skin Restoration)

Polynucleotide injections derived from salmon DNA that repair the skin at a cellular level. The result is genuinely improved skin quality: finer pores, better elasticity, smoother texture. Not available in North America. One of the most requested treatments among Korean women preparing for a major event.

Results peak at 4 to 8 weeks  ·  Ideal 3+ months before your wedding
✦

HIFU and Ultherapy — Non-Surgical Lifting

Focused ultrasound that stimulates collagen deep in the skin, producing a gradual and natural-looking lift of the jawline, brows, and neck. Korean clinics perform more of these in a week than most North American clinics do in a year. The expertise level is in a different category entirely.

Full results at 3 to 6 months  ·  Best booked 6 months before your wedding
✦

Skin Boosters and Juvelook — Glass Skin Hydration

Injectable hydration that plumps the skin from within, creating the lit-from-inside glow that Korean women are known for. Juvelook in particular is a next-generation booster that combines hyaluronic acid with collagen stimulation. Exceptional for bridal skin that needs to look luminous in photography.

Results visible within 2 to 4 weeks  ·  Bookable 6 to 10 weeks before your wedding
✦

Laser Toning and Brightening

Low-fluence laser treatments that address pigmentation, uneven skin tone, and dullness with no downtime. Korean clinics use devices and protocols not yet approved in North America, delivering results in sessions that would take many more treatments at home.

No downtime  ·  Series of 2 to 3 sessions recommended  ·  Bookable 3+ months before
✦

Exosome Therapy

A regenerative treatment that uses growth factors to accelerate skin repair, calm inflammation, and significantly improve skin texture. Often combined with microneedling or laser for enhanced results. Among the most discussed treatments in Korean aesthetic medicine right now.

Results visible within 4 to 6 weeks  ·  Ideal 2 to 3 months before your wedding
