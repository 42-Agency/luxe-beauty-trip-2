Personal Color Analysis, Refined in Seoul

September 2026  ·  4 min read

Personal color analysis did not start in Korea. The idea, matching your natural coloring to the shades that flatter you most, traces back to color theory from the Bauhaus and took off in the West with Carole Jackson's 1980 book "Color Me Beautiful." It reached Korea by way of Japan, where an earlier adaptation had already taken hold. What Korea did with it afterward is the part worth actually knowing.

A Western Idea, Refined for a Wider Range of Skin Tones

Starting in the mid-2010s, dedicated color analysis studios opened across Seoul's fashion neighborhoods, Gangnam, Hongdae, Sinsa-dong, and the method was refined into something more precise, developed specifically to read a wider range of skin undertones than the original Western system was built for. It became less a parlor trick and more a genuine styling discipline.

That refined version is now the one spreading worldwide. It is a fixture on TikTok, and stylists and makeup artists internationally now reference the Korean approach specifically, not the 1980s original. It has become popular enough on its own that people book trips to Seoul specifically for a session, a rare case of a beauty service being the actual reason for the flight, not an add-on to it.

What a Session Actually Looks Like

A session typically takes under an hour. Fabric drapes are held against your face under neutral light, one after another, while a specialist reads how each shifts your complexion, brightening it or draining it, warming it or flattening it. The session ends in a defined seasonal palette, the colors that genuinely work with your natural coloring, rather than the ones you happen to gravitate toward.

What you walk away with applies far beyond that one appointment. Your palette informs your everyday wardrobe, your makeup, and, if a beauty treatment is also part of your trip, it tells you what tones will actually make new work land, brighter skin, a fresh brow shape, a new hair color. It is one of the few beauty services that changes how you shop and dress long after the trip itself is over.

Worth Building Into Your Trip

A color analysis session works best early in a trip, before any styling decisions, a haircut, a color change, new makeup, so you are working from your actual palette rather than guessing. It is a light, non-medical addition that pairs easily alongside a heavier treatment day without competing with it.
