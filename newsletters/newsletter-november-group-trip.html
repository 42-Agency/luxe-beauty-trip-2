November 2026  •  Seoul

The Season to Come to Seoul

People ask us when the best time to plan a beauty trip is, and our answer is nearly always the same. Autumn. The summer heat has gone, the deep cold has not arrived, and Seoul spends a few weeks looking its absolute best. It also happens to be the easiest time of year for skin to settle after treatment. Here is why our small group trip runs in November.
 

The Weather Works in Your Favour

Seoul summers are hot and humid, and that is the hardest environment for freshly treated skin. Heat, sweat, and strong sun all ask for extra care in the days after a session. November is cool and dry instead. It is also far gentler than the deep freeze of January. You can walk comfortably between appointments, keep to a calm routine, and let your skin do its quiet work without fighting the climate.

A Short Window

Fall Foliage at Its Peak

Gold ginkgo and deep red maple run right through the city in November. The palace grounds, the lanes of Bukchon, the paths along Seoul Forest, and the temple stairs on Namsan all turn colour at once. The display peaks in the second half of the month, which is exactly when the trip is scheduled. It is a beautiful backdrop for the slower, restful days we build around your treatments.

Late November

The First Christmas Lights

By the later departures, Seoul is already dressing for the holidays. The department store windows, the light installations along Cheonggyecheon stream, and the big displays around Myeongdong and Gangnam all go up before December. Evenings out feel festive without any of the crowding that comes later in the season. If you love a city at Christmas, this is a lovely time to see one.

How It Feels

Wellness That Feels Like a Reset

A jjimjilbang soak, a quiet tea house, an unhurried morning. These land differently in autumn. In summer the heat undoes the calm almost as soon as you step outside. In the depth of winter, going anywhere is an effort. In November the air is crisp, the light is soft, and a slow day actually feels slow. Guests tell us they go home feeling genuinely rested, not just treated.
